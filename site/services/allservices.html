What we offer

FAQ

Common questions

How long does a website take?

It depends on the number of pages and features. A standard business website usually takes 2 to 4 weeks from approved content. You get a timeline before work starts.

Can we update the website ourselves?

Yes. We add a simple admin or CMS so your team can change text, images, products and posts.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. We review what's working, fix speed and search issues, and move your content to the new design.
