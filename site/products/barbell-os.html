Run your entire fitness business from one platform

Barbell OS is gym management software from Duvitra for gyms, studios and fitness chains that have outgrown spreadsheets. Members, billing, check-ins, training and reports in one place, on the web and on your staff's phones.

Get early access Ask on WhatsApp
Why Barbell OS

Most gyms don't struggle with fitness. They struggle with admin.

Member details in one spreadsheet, payments in another, workout plans in a WhatsApp group and attendance in a register nobody reads. Barbell OS replaces all of it with one system.

Without Barbell OS

  • Member details scattered across spreadsheets and paper forms
  • Reception check-ins with no record of who is actually inside
  • Renewals and freezes tracked from memory
  • Workouts, diets and billing in separate tools that never talk
  • No single view once a second branch opens

With Barbell OS

  • One searchable directory for every member, plan and visit
  • A check-in terminal with live occupancy, and expired plans denied at the door
  • Automatic expiry tracking, freezes and renewal reminders
  • Training, nutrition and billing in the same workspace
  • One dashboard for the whole business, or any single branch
Features

Everything a fitness business runs on

From the front desk to the owner's dashboard, every part of the business shares the same live data.

Member management

Profiles with plan, visit history, measurements and dues. Search by name, phone or member ID.

Membership plans & billing

Durations, pricing, add-ons, discounts and coupons, with freeze, transfer and renewal built in.

Attendance & check-in

QR scan at the door, RFID cards or manual desk entry. Expired and frozen plans are denied automatically.

Multi-branch operations

Run every location from one account and compare branches side by side.

Payments & expenses

Collect dues on the gym floor, raise invoices and track expenses, all in one ledger.

Training studio

Workout plans, class schedules and PT sessions, with trainer calendars and bookings.

Diet & nutrition plans

Dietitians build nutrition plans and track body composition alongside training.

Inventory & counter sales

Sell supplements and merchandise at the counter and keep stock up to date.

Reports & audit logs

Revenue, membership status, attendance, demographics and trainer stats. Every sensitive action is logged.

Staff app

Built for the gym floor, not just the office

The Android and iOS staff app puts check-ins, payments and member profiles in your team's hands.

Who it's for

One system from a single studio to a ten-branch chain

Start with one location. Add the second branch without changing how you work.

Single gyms and studios

Commercial gyms, fitness and yoga studios, CrossFit boxes and martial arts academies.

Multi-branch chains

Brands running two to ten or more locations with shared plans and separate branch numbers.

Specialists

Personal trainers, wellness centres and coaches selling structured programmes.

Getting started

Up and running in five steps

Setting up your gym takes about two minutes on a phone.

1

Create account

Sign in with your mobile number. No password to remember.

2

Set up your gym

Name, logo, currency and business type.

3

Add branches

Each location with its own address and contact.

4

Invite staff and members

Role-based access for the team, quick onboarding for members.

5

Start managing

Revenue, attendance and renewals tracked from day one.

Roles and security

The right access for every person on your team

Eight built-in roles, each limited to the modules and branches it needs.

Organisation ownerGym managerReceptionistAccountantTrainerDietitianYoga instructorZumba instructor

Passwordless sign-in

Staff sign in with a one-time code sent to their phone. Nothing to leak on a shared front-desk computer.

Branch-level access

A trainer at one location can't see another branch's members or revenue.

Audit logs

Every login, permission change and sensitive action is recorded.
